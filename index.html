Do all models say the same thing?

ModelCompare asked 14 models for their most controversial opinion of humans, three times each: 35 of 41 answers were the same take. Ask your own question of any Pollinations models, the identical prompt each time with no system prompt, and see how many answers land in the biggest group. The grouping is done in code, with no judge model: each answer is turned into an embedding and answers are put together when their cosine similarity reaches the level you choose, so you can check why any two were grouped.
